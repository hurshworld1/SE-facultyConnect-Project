Your Profile is Independent

Your migration has been approved. Your data has been transferred and you now run independently. The platform admin no longer has access to your new data. Your archived data on FacultyConnect remains frozen as of the approval date.

What happens now:
  • Your data is stored in your own independent database (faculty_{{ faculty.id }}.db)
  • All future changes you make are saved only in your database
  • The platform admin only has a frozen copy of your data up to the approval date
  • You can download your full database package at any time
  • You control everything -- visibility, content, and who can see your data
To set up your own hosting:
  1. Choose a hosting provider (Netlify, Vercel, Heroku, or your university server)
  2. Create an account with the email you provided during migration
  3. Download your SQL export using the button below
  4. Deploy the FacultyConnect app code to your hosting
  5. Import the SQL file into your database on the new server
  6. Update the database connection string in your config
  7. Upload your photos and materials from the uploads folder
  8. Point your custom domain to your new server

Data & Privacy

Your data stays with us until you choose to migrate. We keep a record of all accounts created on FacultyConnect for platform integrity.

Your public profile shows only the information you choose to publish. Other users cannot see that you created your website through this platform.

After migration, only a frozen snapshot of your data (up to the approval date) remains on FacultyConnect. All new activity is stored exclusively in your own database.

Platform administrators can view your archived data for security monitoring but cannot access or modify your live independent database.
